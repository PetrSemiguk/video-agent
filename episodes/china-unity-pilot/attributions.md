## Geographic vectors

Made with Natural Earth (v5.1.2, public domain). Yellow River and Yangtze are
existing vap.geo.river source-to-mouth outputs. They depict geographic context,
not a reconstruction of ancient channels. No historical polity boundaries or
Grand Canal route geometry are drawn.
