## Geographic vectors

Made with Natural Earth (v5.1.2, public domain). The Yellow, Yangtze, Hai, Huai and
Qiantang (mapped as Fuchun) are vap.geo.river outputs. They depict geographic context,
not a reconstruction of ancient channels. The Huai east of about 116.4 E is extended
by hand to Hongze Lake.

Terrain masks of the Tibetan Plateau and the eastern lowlands are our own contours of
the terrain model credited above, clipped to Natural Earth land.
