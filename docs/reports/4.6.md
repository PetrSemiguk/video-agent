**Grand Canal routes,** `vap.geo.route`, each with `<slug>.sources.md` and `approximate: true`:

| Route | Shape | Drawn length | Simplified |
|---|---|---:|---|
| `grand-canal-sui` | Y, hub at Luoyang: Hangzhou → Yangzhou → Luoyang, plus Luoyang → Beijing area | 1,106 + 683 km | **The northern branch has only its endpoints confirmed, so it is one straight dashed line.** The real canal ran farther east. Chenliu → Luoyang is also straight |
| `grand-canal-yuan` | Hangzhou → Beijing via Jining and Linqing | 1,363 km | Straight between named cities. Huai'an → Jining goes through Xuzhou only. Some nodes come from descriptions of later courses |

Only one leg snaps to a Natural Earth river: Tianjin northward along the Hai. "Wuqing" there is my routing point, not a
place the sources name, and the sources file says so. The modern Yellow River is not snapped to, as it is not the
13th-century channel. UNESCO and Britannica returned 403 to my fetcher, so the routes rest on Wikipedia and a travel guide.

**Five river systems: rivers, not basins.** HydroBASINS is not clean. Commercial use is allowed, but its license
(Technical Documentation v1.4, Appendix A) requires written end-user terms as protective as its own and two years of
inspectable records, makes the licensee indemnify WWF, and lets WWF terminate at its sole discretion. As the roadmap
directs, nothing was downloaded and the rivers light up in order. Recorded in `docs/licenses.md`. The screen says
"5 river systems", because rivers are what is drawn; the narration keeps "five major river basins".

River data built by hand in part:

- `hai`: runs through the mapped Sanggan and Yongding, 631 km. The Hai alone is a 93 km stub, which read as a speck.
- `qiantang`: Natural Earth names this reach "Fuchun".
- `huai`: Natural Earth stops at about 116.4° E, so `huai.extend.json` carries it to Hongze Lake through three towns.

**`LowerThird`:** width from content; `placement: "auto"` picks the first corner clear of `subject`, sampled along the
camera path for the card's whole life.

**`StatCounter`:** number and unit on one line, one line of meaning, the date on its own line.

**`TitleCard` v2:** titles up to 24 characters at 184 px on the map, no card, slow push-in.

**Motion rule:** `vap.episode build` fails, naming the shot and time, on any stretch over 2 s below 0.03 px/frame: from the
camera plan in the preview stage, from the video in the render stage. `hold` passes only with a reason.

**Speed limit, added after the first review:** `motion.camera.maxSpeedPx` = 9 px/frame. The value is the rule of thumb of 7
seconds to cross the frame (1920 / (7 × 30) = 9.1), which I know as a cinematography convention and did not look up. Our own
evidence supports it: labels read at the lowlands pass (8.9), and did not at the earlier 14. `followLine` now chases its aim
point within the limit, and the build fails with the shot's name if any preset goes over it, with 0.5 of tolerance.

Smaller additions: `RiverLine.fromFraction`; `HistoricalLayer.only`, `color` and `exitAt`; `PlaceLabel.exitAt`; `hold.reason`;
`SectionTag`, `BigDate` and `KeyFact` fit their content.
