# Step 5.3 — Storyboarder

Status: **complete by the reviewer's PASS WITH NOTES, with one criterion for the owner.** On (a), "at least as good as v2", the reviewer says "only partly supported".

## Built

- **`.claude/agents/storyboarder.md`:** script and words to `storyboard.json`, with a shot plan, data preparation, a generator, and a loop of validator, motion check, preview sheet and self-check, at most 2 revision rounds. `studio/scripts/storyboard-kit.cjs` holds the helpers its generators use.
- **Rhythm** (`docs/visual-grammar.md`, `vap.qa.rhythm`): the preview stage reports and warns. By the owner's decision the rule of 1-3 cuts per minute counts **hard cuts** only, as the viewer sees them: a change of plate, a transition, or a break in the camera's motion. Over 3 per minute is a violation, and a warning in the build. `shots_per_minute` is reported apart, with no limit.
- **Claims on screen** (owner's decision): a layer stating a fact needs `claims`, ids from `facts.md`. Validation fails without them or on an unknown id. It checks that the id exists; that the wording stays within the claim remains the author's and the fact-checker's job. Place names and navigation are exempt, unless they carry a number.
- **Lines fade out** (owner's decision): `RiverLine` and `RouteLine` fade over `motion.frames.base` at the end of their layer. `exitFrames: 0` turns it off.

**Fixes needed on the way:**

- **Alignment:** `1,500` from Whisper reads as "fifteen hundred" for timing. `vap/align/spellings.json` lists how Whisper spells names ("Atbarah", "Nassar"): an explicit list, not fuzzy matching. Timings still come only from the audio. "In the rest" was heard as "And the rest", so that one chunk has a new seed in `voice.job.json`.
- **`vap.geo.river`** wrote a bare `Infinity`, invalid JSON, for a river far from water. It now writes null, and the two affected files are corrected.
