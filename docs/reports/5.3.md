## Owner's fourth round: pan and zoom apart, a wide shot per section, and the `reading` shot

### 1. The speed limit is now two limits

One figure of 9 px/frame covered pan and zoom together, so a change of scale inside a short shot
broke it. Pan and zoom are now limited apart, because the eye follows them differently: a pan moves
every pixel by the same amount, a zoom leaves the centre still and moves the corners most.

| What | Limit, px/frame | Where it comes from |
|---|---:|---|
| Pan | 9 | "7 seconds to cross the frame", 1920 / (7 × 30) |
| Zoom, at the corners | 16 | Averaged over the frame (RMS radius = diagonal / √12 = 0.577 of the corner radius) 16 at the corners is 9.2, the pan limit |
| Zoom while a text layer is on screen | 9 | Reading needs a steady frame; pilot v2 showed a label unreadable at 14 |

The camera plan now carries `labelFrames`, the frame ranges where a text layer is up, so the strict
limit applies to exactly those frames and a fast flyover without labels does not pay for them.
`MapAttribution` is deliberately not a label: it is on every map frame and is secondary, so counting
it would mean the strict limit always applies. A test makes every registered component declare
itself map, text, or neither, so a new component cannot slip through unclassified.

`followLine` keeps to both limits by itself; a shot that shows text anywhere keeps the strict zoom
limit throughout, since the preset limits a whole shot while the build checks each frame.

Egypt now measures **peak pan 6.32, peak zoom 5.80** px/frame, against the old combined figure of
9.49 which sat right on the 9.5 edge. Labels are up for 3,926 of 5,271 frames, so the strict limit
governs about 74% of the episode and the headroom is real where it matters.

**One test changed, and it is worth saying why.** `a shot over the speed limit fails the check and
names itself` built an artificial fast shot at zoom 5.5, while the `egypt` plate stays sharp only to
5.5599 and the camera spline overshot to 5.5601. The plate check therefore fired before the speed
check. The new keys of the limited `followLine` in the next shot nudged that spline by about 1e-4
and tipped it. The shot is now a fast pan at its own zoom, inside the plate, so the test exercises
the speed rule it is named after.

### 2. A wide shot in every section

New rhythm rule: each `## section` needs at least one wide shot (frame ≥ 4,500 km), so it places its
subject before going close. It reports `wide_shots` per section and warns when a section has none.

Egypt's `ethiopia` shot was pulled from zoom 5.10 to 4.95 (4,181 → 4,637 km) at the sentence "The
river brings its water from far to the south, mostly from Ethiopia". That is where The Answer
belongs at basin scale, and it broke a run of six regional shots into three.

**"Wide" is relative to the plates, by the owner's decision.** An absolute 4,500 km called a
one-country plate narrow however far the camera pulled back: the `egypt` plate centred on Egypt
tops out near 4,210 km. A shot is now wide when its frame is within **90% of the widest safe frame
its own plate can show at that latitude** — the plate's cover zoom, one safe-area step inside it.
The ground width stays in the report as the absolute reference (`frame_width_km`, with
`wide_from_km` beside it). A second warning fires when the widest map shot is under **2×** the
narrowest, which is the honest signal for "shot at one scale".

**The rule says Egypt never pulls back, and all four sections warn.** Under it the episode has
**zero** wide shots, where the absolute rule counted two:

| Shot | Frame, km | Wide from, km | Short by |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| `ethiopia` (basin) | 4,637 | 5,165 | 10% |
| `share-86` (basin) | 4,519 | 5,168 | 13% |
| `south` (egypt) | 3,076 | 3,922 | 22% |
| `open` (egypt) | 2,185 | 3,787 | 42% |

Scale spread is 3.67× (1,264 to 4,637 km), so the spread warning does not fire. Reaching "wide"
means re-framing one shot per section: `open` to about zoom 5.1, `share-86` and `ethiopia` to about
4.80. **For the owner:** that is four framings and a render, and it changes how the episode opens,
so I did not do it on my own. Say the word and it is a short job.

### 3. Memory is now checked before a heavy render

The render after the `ethiopia` change reached 30% and was killed because the machine was low on
memory. Out of memory a render is killed, not slowed, so `vap.episode build` now asks first
(`vap.system`): it reads the available physical memory, refuses below **6 GB** with the figure in
the message, and passes `--concurrency` to `render-episode.mjs`, which takes one worker per 2.5 GB
above that floor, capped at 2 and defaulting to 1. The episode render already used one worker, and
writes to `…partial.mp4` before renaming, which is why the killed run left the previous MP4 intact.
The rule is in `AGENTS.md`. A pytest fixture stubs the reading, so how busy the machine happens to
be cannot decide whether the tests pass; `test_system.py` exercises the real one.

`studio/out/episode-egypt-nile-synced.mp4` is therefore still one change behind: it has the split
limits and the `reading` fix, but `ethiopia` at its old zoom. At the time of writing the machine had
**5.23 GB** available, under the floor, so the render waits. The preview stage, the motion gate, the
rhythm report and every test cover the current storyboard.
