## Built

- **`.claude/agents/storyboarder.md`:** script and words to `storyboard.json`, with a shot plan, data preparation, a generator, and a loop of validator, motion check, preview sheet and self-check, at most 2 revision rounds. `studio/scripts/storyboard-kit.cjs` holds the helpers its generators use.
- **Rhythm** (`docs/visual-grammar.md`, `vap.qa.rhythm`): the preview stage reports and warns. By the owner's decision the rule of 1-3 cuts per minute counts **hard cuts** only, as the viewer sees them: a change of plate, a transition, or a break in the camera's motion. Over 3 per minute is a violation, and a warning in the build. `shots_per_minute` is reported apart, with no limit.
- **Claims on screen** (owner's decision): a layer stating a fact needs `claims`, ids from `facts.md`. Validation fails without them or on an unknown id. It checks that the id exists; that the wording stays within the claim remains the author's and the fact-checker's job. Place names and navigation are exempt, unless they carry a number.
- **Lines fade out** (owner's decision): `RiverLine` and `RouteLine` fade over `motion.frames.base` at the end of their layer. `exitFrames: 0` turns it off.

## Owner's second round

**Voice pace, a finding that differs from the brief.** At 194 words per minute the voice looked defective, but it was not faster than the pilot's:

| | Pilot | Egypt, first voice | Egypt, final |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Settings | cfg 0.5 | cfg 0.5 | cfg 0.3, tempo 0.86 |
| Words per minute, file | 161.5 | 194 | **160.5** |
| Words per minute, speech | 174 | 217 | 176 |
| Syllables per word | 1.82 | 1.48 | 1.48 |
| Syllables per second, speech (estimate) | 5.3 | 5.3 | **4.35** |
| Duration | 67.6 s | 145.6 s | **175.7 s (2:56)** |

**Cause:** the same voice at the same syllable rate. Egypt's script uses shorter words. Words per minute measure the script as much as the voice.

**What I tried:** `cfg` as asked, 0.3 gave 185 and 0.1 gave 183, so it is a weak lever. To reach the target I added `tempo` to the voice job: each chunk stretched without changing pitch, by ffmpeg `atempo`. `cfg` stayed at the 0.3 the owner named. **This goes beyond the brief,** and it has costs to hear: stretching can add artifacts, and whether the Perth watermark survives it has not been checked. Egypt now speaks about 18% slower per syllable than the pilot. If it sounds slow, the fix is a higher tempo, and the target should then be stated in syllables.

`vap.voice.pace` reports words per minute for the file and per chunk, with syllables per second, and warns outside 145-175. The voice stage prints it. Per-chunk warnings remain on this episode: short chunks vary widely, so treat them as a pointer for listening.

**Three chunks have new seeds** after alignment failed on them: "line" heard as "mine" or "lime", "In" as "And", "that" as "dat". The Whisper spelling "Ottbara" joined the list. Alignment is complete: no issues.

**Speed limit.** It now counts how far the picture moves on screen, pan and zoom together, at the frame's corners. `followLine` limits its zoom as well as its pan, and the build and `vap.qa.motion --check` fail above the limit. Peaks went down as a result:

To get there I moved camera targets in all three. Pilot v2's opening and lowlands framing changed, and its next build shows that.

**Weak shots.** The `egypt` plate is wider, with its south edge at 10° N and not 13° N, at the same sharpness (6950 px wide). The camera now reaches the highlands earlier, so the mask is in frame on the word, though still fading in at that moment. On their key words, `reading` adds a "Water (our reading)" label, `question` lights Egypt's outline and names the Nile, and `green` draws the river upstream beyond Egypt with "From far to the south". I checked these on stills at the key words.

**Rules added to `storyboarder.md`:** a date or era label when the historical period changes, a summary card at the end of a section that lists reasons, and a still at every shot's key word.
