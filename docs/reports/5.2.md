**Why the fact-check needed so many rounds.** The checker opens more sources than the research did, so each round found issues the earlier ones could not. It also searches anew each time, and the two-source
rule then caught figures the research had let through. This was useful, and also expensive: about 85,000-100,000 tokens per round. Stage 5.5 should expect this.

**`research.md` is corrected:** a block at its top lists what the fact-check found wrong, including the 99% (which covers the valley and Delta, not only the river banks), and which figures are not for narration.
The fact-checker's added sources are registered in `docs/licenses.md`. `research.md` and the sources in `fact-check.md` differ in numbering by design: A sources are the fact-checker's.

- **Map needs for step 5.3:** a wider plate for Ethiopia, the highlands mask, and a check that Lake Nasser is in Natural Earth's lakes. No polygon of the cultivated strip exists; the script relies on the plate showing it.
- **Rule 9 of `visual-grammar.md`** wants a number's date on its own line, while the share graphic now has no date. The storyboard must settle it.
