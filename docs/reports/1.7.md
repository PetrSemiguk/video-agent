### Owner's license decisions

**HDX: kept.** HDX publishes COD-AB Ukraine and Costa Rica under **CC BY-IGO 3.0**; the OHM ways say
CC BY 4.0. `docs/licenses.md` names the source's license and records the tag as a discrepancy. The
credit follows the source's terms: title, source, link, license link, changes made and no implied
endorsement. In 1914 these are 36 ways of the Russian Empire and Austria-Hungary. The tag still
decides whether a way passes: a way counts as allowed because it is tagged CC BY 4.0, and the source
table then supplies the real terms.

**Slovenia: verified at the source.** The [GURS general terms of 2022-09-19](https://www.e-prostor.gov.si/fileadmin/struktura/EGP/preberi_me.pdf)
allow non-commercial and commercial use, sharing and adaptation, with the source credited, and the
[public access page](https://www.e-prostor.gov.si/dostopi/javni-dostop/) says the same. The UNVERIFIED
status was therefore not needed. The earlier wording from the OSM wiki was replaced by the form these
terms require: `Geodetska uprava Republike Slovenije, Evidenca državne meje, stanje najkasneje 2026-05`.

**One limit remains.** The terms ask for the date of the data. OHM does not record it for these ways, so
the credit gives an upper bound: their latest edit in OHM, May 2026. Evidenca državne meje is my
reading of the ways' `National border record`, and that collection is listed in the terms.

## 2. Errata

`<layer>.errata.json` is hand-kept beside each layer and regeneration leaves it alone.
`npm run validate:storyboard` and `vap.episode build` both refuse a shot using a layer with a blocker,
unless its `errataOverride` gives a reason. To make that possible a shot needs a way to name a layer,
so a new production component was added: **`HistoricalLayer`**. It is in the catalog and the JSON

## 3. Cliopatria

- **License:** CC BY 4.0, from its LICENSE.md. The data paper is also CC BY 4.0.
- **Origin:** a set of 508 map images drawn by hand from published maps since 2014, converted to
  polygons by script, then reviewed and edited by Seshat researchers.
- **Limits:** whole-year frames between source images, no border uncertainty, and only "one version"
  of each territory, as its README says. The polygons are smooth at this scale.
- **Version:** pinned to v0.2.1 and checked by SHA-256. RELATION rows are left out, since they would
  overlap their members.

The main pitfall is frame lag. A frame stays valid until the next image, so frame 555-560 still shows
Western Wei and Liang (gone in 557), frame 587-591 still shows Chen (gone in 589), and frame 1642-1644
shows Ming in Beijing. The frames just after are right, so `--frame-year` selects them on purpose. A
useful surprise: Cliopatria has Shun as "Li Zicheng" and Southern Ming, which OHM lacks.

## 4. Composer

`uv run python -m vap.geo.hist compose --plate <p> --spec <file.yaml>` builds a layer from Natural
Earth units, features of other layers and custom polygons, with subtraction. Overlapping states stop
compilation and name the pair. Borders are noded first, so neighbours share exact edges and
`coverage_simplify` works.

The example uses Cliopatria frame 561 for all four states, including Western Liang, so nothing is
drawn by hand. Its dates are whole, well-known years. I confirmed each cited page opens, not its full text.
Confidence is medium for three states and low for Western Liang, whose contour at about 70,000 km²
looks generous. The guide is in `docs/maps.md`.
